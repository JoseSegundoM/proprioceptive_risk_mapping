## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-30

First public version, with the manuscript prepared for IEEE-EPIC 2026.

### Added
- Monte Carlo simulator: map generator, belief updates M0-M3, risk-aware A*, paired statistics.
- MuJoCo capture on parametrised terrain, for the legged and wheeled models, and a terrain classifier.
- Mission records of experiments E1-E8, statistics, figures and the LaTeX manuscript.
- Tests, automatic checks of the manuscript, and continuous integration.
